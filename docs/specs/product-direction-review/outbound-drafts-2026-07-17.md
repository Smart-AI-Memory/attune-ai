>    figure out where the people who'd actually use this are, and

**Voice:** first-person "I" per `user_authorial_voice_first_person`.
**Honesty constraint (§7):** this asks for users, it does not claim
them. No download figures — the attune-rag 27k/month number is

**Risk to weigh before posting:** this is publicly admitting a thin
user base. That is on-brand for the discipline article's honesty
posture and probably earns more trust than it costs — but it is a
public, durable statement, and that is your call, not mine.

> I've been building attune-ai in the open for months. Here's a number

> someone who ran it on their own repo.

> So I'm asking directly instead of waiting: **if you've ever pip

> broke, what confused you, and whether you'd ever run it again.
>
> Especially if you bounced. *Particularly* if you bounced — that's
> the conversation I need most and the one I'm least likely to get.

> Reply here or DM me.

**Why:** it is the only other real list. Nine people is small enough

count that honestly as a non-conversation.

**Do not** mass-DM identical text; GitHub reads that as spam and it
would earn a reputation hit worth more than the data.

> Hi — you starred attune-ai a while back, so I'm taking a chance that
> you looked at it more closely than most.
>
> I'm trying to talk to people who actually ran it on a real repo
> (rather than guessing from download counts, which tell me nothing).
> Did you ever get as far as installing it?
>
> If yes: what broke? If no: what made you not bother? Both answers
> are useful — the second one maybe more.
>
> Genuinely no obligation, and I'm not selling anything.
