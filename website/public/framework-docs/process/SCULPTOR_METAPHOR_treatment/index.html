    The Sculptor and the Adaptive Chisel — internal deployment guide¶

    Status: INTERNAL deployment guide (relabeled 2026-07-16 after Patrick chose "carve it" via pushback form) — NOT reader-facing. The reader-facing artifact is the one-image chapter seed: SCULPTOR_CHAPTER_SEED.md. This doc holds the full clause set (ratified in-session 2026-07-16: sculpt once, cast many; the strengthened chisel bound; the grain-over-encyclopedia clause) as guidance for how WE deploy the metaphor — the clauses become chapter PROSE, never metaphor baggage. Scope: the craft-chapter frame for the book. The published "colleague" frame (discipline article) remains the public relationship metaphor — this complements it, never replaces it.

    The metaphor¶

    The marble is data — a body of data and information contains many latent forms; the discipline chooses which manifests. The human and the AI are co-sculptors with asymmetric chisels: the human decides what the form is; the agent carves fast and shows every stroke.

    Sculpting is the counter-move to additive slop: most of the work is removal — chipping away everything that isn't the statue. (Stock receipts: the 10.0.0 deletion release; the 3,388-LOC EmpathyOS removal; D19, where a live pushback form removed a feature before it was written.)

    Sculpt once, cast many¶

    Two distinct moves, never conflated:

    • Carving — deciding form from the data. Creative, subtractive, human-led, with the adaptive chisel assisting.
    • Casting — replicating a decided form across surfaces. Mechanical, deterministic, fully delegated.

    Masters are sculpted; projections are cast. This is the literal architecture (FormSchema → renderers; feature masters → help projections; the article source → built page) and it explains why projector work is safe to automate and master work is not: form decisions live in the carving, never in the cast.

    The chisel never chooses the statue¶

    Adaptation is scoped to technique — edge, angle, pressure — never to intent. Not a courtesy; structural. An unbounded adaptive tool doesn't fail loudly — it drifts: a thousand small technique adjustments each shade into micro-decisions about form, and you end up with a statue nobody chose. That is the real failure mode of AI-assisted work, and it never announces itself.

    Three mechanisms keep the bound real (a bound with no enforcement is a wish):

    1. Formulas — the contract written in math. Gates that pin intent-level decisions to the human and fail loudly on breach: the spend gate, the referent gate, budget caps, drift-guard tests. A formula is a receipt that checks itself forever.
    2. Receipts — every stroke shown. The chisel's work is inspectable at stroke level, so drift is visible before it compounds.
    3. Voice, not hand. When the chisel learns something that argues the statue itself should be different — the grain reveals a flaw, or a better form — it does not re-aim the work. It surfaces: a formal pushback with a concrete alternative, and the human picks. Advocacy on intent is welcome; decision on intent never transfers. (Receipt: D19 — the chisel argued against carving a fifth construct, and the statue got simpler.)

    Grain over encyclopedia¶

    The chisel adapts from encyclopedic knowledge checked against the actual grain — introspect, dogfood, then carve. The grain overrules the encyclopedia, every time. (This clause is what makes the encyclopedic-knowledge claim honest: confabulated signatures and stale API knowledge are documented failure modes; verification against the live material is the whetstone.)

    Formulas — scoped honestly¶

    "Receipts expressed as formulas" applies to the gates (coverage floors, budget caps, drift guards — genuinely formulaic). Demonstrations remain the receipt class for the rest (a response id from a human click, a live render in a second host, a dogfood run). Never let the formula frame launder precision that measurement didn't deliver.

    Deployment rules (binding)¶

    • Never deploy the metaphor without a receipt attached (D19 and the deletion release are the stock pair).
    • Always name the marble as data/the solution in the same breath — the drift-back reading (AI = clay) contradicts the colleague thesis and must be pre-empted at every use.
    • State the chisel bound explicitly whenever the adaptive chisel appears, or the metaphor will be quoted against you.
    • Debut in long-form (the book's craft chapter), not LinkedIn; colleague stays the public frame until the discipline brand matures.
